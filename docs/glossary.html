Glossary — Champion Plumbing

Promotional and educational content about Champion Plumbing. Not operated by Champion Plumbing.

  • Emergency Residential Plumbing: Emergency residential plumbing, called 24-Hour Residential Plumbing or urgent home plumbing, is the rapid diagnosis and repair of water system failures in homes.
  • Slab Leak Detection: Acoustic listening equipment and pressure testing are typically used to locate water leaks beneath a concrete building slab without demolishing the floor, an approach sometimes called foundation leak detection that aims to pinpoint the source of the leak precisely.
  • Sewer Line Emergency Repair: Sewer line emergency repair, called emergency sewer repair, is rapid intervention to resolve urgent failures in a building's main sewer line, covering pipe collapses, severe blockages, root intrusions, joint separations and active sewage backups that cannot wait for a scheduled appointment.
  • Acoustic Leak Detection: Acoustic leak detection, called Sonic Leak Detection, is a non-invasive method that locates water leaks by detecting, amplifying, and analyzing the acoustic signals a pressurized pipe produces when water escapes through a crack, joint failure, or corroded section.
  • Localized Pipe Repair: Localized pipe repair, called a targeted pipe fix, addresses damage within a single, defined section of pipe.
  • Main Water Shut-off Valve: A main water shut-off valve, called a water shutoff valve, is a device that stops the flow of water into a building's entire plumbing system by closing off the supply line at the point where it enters the structure, covering every fixture, appliance, pipe and water-using system inside the home or commercial property in a single operation.
  • Grease Traps: Grease traps, called FOG interceptors or grease interceptors, are plumbing devices installed between kitchen drain lines and the municipal sewer system.
  • Burst Pipe Emergency: A burst pipe emergency, called a pipe burst or water line rupture, is an uncontrolled release of water from a damaged pipe that requires immediate action to stop structural damage, mold growth, flooding of living spaces, contamination of the water supply and destruction of flooring, drywall, insulation and personal property.
  • Clogged Drain Emergency: A clogged drain emergency, called a Blocked Drain Crisis, is a sudden complete or near-complete blockage that causes immediate water backup and prevents normal drainage flow.
  • Overflowing Toilet Emergency: An overflowing toilet emergency, called a bathroom flooding emergency, is a plumbing failure in which water discharges over the rim of the toilet bowl onto the surrounding floor.
  • No Hot Water Emergency: A no hot water emergency, called a hot water failure or domestic hot water failure, is a sudden and complete loss of a building's hot water supply.
  • Flooded Basement: A flooded basement, called water in basement or basement flooding, is an accumulation of unwanted water in the lowest level of a building.
  • Backflow Prevention Standards: Backflow Prevention Standards, called Backflow Codes or Cross-Connection Control Regulations, are a type of plumbing regulation that defines the design, installation, testing and maintenance requirements for devices that stop contaminated water from flowing backward into a clean supply.
  • Oklahoma Plumbing Emergency Code: The Oklahoma Plumbing Emergency Code, known as OK Plumbing Law, establishes official regulations for the design, installation, and repair of plumbing systems in emergency situations across Oklahoma.
  • Plumbing Emergency Response: Plumbing Emergency Response, called Emergency Plumbing Assistance, is a type of Emergency Service that addresses urgent, unplanned plumbing failures in residential and commercial properties, covering burst pipes, active water leaks, sewer backups, gas line failures, slab leaks, drain blockages and loss of water supply before the damage spreads.
  • Same Day Plumbing Service: Same day plumbing service, sometimes called expedited plumbing service, generally refers to urgent repairs such as burst pipes, slab leaks, gas line failures, clogged drains, broken water heaters, toilet failures and active water leaks that may need prompt attention to help prevent structural damage.
  • Google Guaranteed Plumbing Service: Google Guaranteed Plumbing Service, called a vetted plumbing service, is a certification Google issues to local plumbing contractors who pass a multi-step screening process.
  • BBB Accredited Plumbing Service: A BBB Accredited Plumbing Service is a type of business certification issued by the Better Business Bureau to plumbing contractors who meet defined standards for trustworthiness, transparency, complaint handling and ethical business conduct.
  • Continuous Feed Garbage Disposal: A continuous feed garbage disposal, called a continuous flow disposer, grinds food waste while the unit runs.
  • Batch Feed Garbage Disposal: A batch feed garbage disposal, called a batch-feed disposer, is a type of garbage disposal that grinds one measured load of food waste at a time, activated only after a lid is placed over the drain opening, covering the grinding chamber, flywheel with impellers, magnetic cover switch and motor inside a sealed unit before any grinding begins.
  • 1/3 HP Garbage Disposal: A 1/3 HP Garbage Disposal, called a 1/3 HP Waste Disposer, mounts beneath a kitchen sink and uses a 1/3 horsepower electric motor to grind food scraps, soft vegetable peels, small fruit rinds, and similar organic waste into fine particles that flush through the drain line.
  • 1/2 HP Garbage Disposal: A 1/2 HP garbage disposal, called a 1/2 HP disposer or half-horsepower garbage disposer, is a single-stage electric food waste unit that mounts beneath a kitchen sink drain, grinds soft to moderately fibrous food scraps into fine particles and flushes them through the drain line.
  • 3/4 HP Garbage Disposal: A 3/4 HP garbage disposal, called a 3/4 HP Food Waste Disposer, is a continuous feed kitchen appliance powered by a 0.75 horsepower induction motor.
  • Waste King Garbage Disposal: Waste King Garbage Disposal, sold as the Waste King disposer, is a kitchen appliance that grinds food waste into fine particles small enough to pass through standard drain plumbing.
  • Septic Safe Garbage Disposal: A septic safe garbage disposal, called a septic-friendly disposer, is a food waste disposer built to grind kitchen scraps into fine particles small enough to pass through a septic system without disrupting the bacterial treatment process, clogging the drain field, or overloading the tank with solids.
  • Stainless Steel Grind System: A stainless steel grind system, called a Heavy-Duty Grind System, is a grinding mechanism inside a garbage disposal that pulverizes food waste through a multi-stage assembly of a stainless steel flywheel, swivel impellers and a grind ring, reducing solid scraps to fine particles that flush safely through the drain line.
  • Anti-Jam Garbage Disposal Technology: Anti-Jam Garbage Disposal Technology is a motor control system built into a garbage disposal unit that automatically detects, reverses or pulses the motor to clear obstructions before they stall the grind plate, covering torque monitoring, impeller direction switching, jam detection response and motor stress relief across residential and commercial kitchen applications.
  • Sound Insulation Garbage Disposal: Sound insulation garbage disposal is a built-in noise reduction component.
  • Garbage Disposal Stainless Steel Grind Chamber: The garbage disposal stainless steel grind chamber, called a grinding chamber, is the enclosed, bowl-shaped cavity inside a garbage disposal unit.
  • Mounting System (Garbage Disposal): A mounting system (garbage disposal), called a disposal mounting assembly, is a plumbing component that secures a garbage disposal unit to the underside of a sink drain opening.
  • Gasket Seal (Garbage Disposal): A gasket seal (garbage disposal), called a disposal gasket, is a rubber ring that creates a watertight seal between the garbage disposal unit and the sink flange, preventing water from leaking down through the mounting assembly and into the cabinet below.
  • Drain Flange: A drain flange, called a disposal flange, is a garbage disposal part that connects and seals a garbage disposal or standard drain to the sink opening, bridging the gap between the sink basin and the drain assembly below.
  • P-Trap Connection: A P-Trap Connection, called a P-Trap Fitting, is a curved plumbing component that connects a drain outlet to the waste line and holds a standing water seal to block sewer gases from entering a building.
  • Dishwasher Drain Inlet: The dishwasher drain inlet, called the dishwasher drain port, is the dedicated port on a garbage disposal body where the dishwasher drain hose attaches, routing wastewater from the dishwasher tub through the disposal and out to the main drain.
  • Wall Switch (Garbage Disposal): A wall switch (garbage disposal), called a kitchen disposal switch or garbage disposer switch, is a type of electrical switch mounted on the kitchen wall that controls power to a garbage disposal unit by completing or breaking the circuit between the disposal motor and the household electrical supply.
  • Loud Garbage Disposal Operation: Loud garbage disposal operation, called a grinding disposal sound, is a condition in which the unit produces excessive noise during normal food waste grinding.
  • Electrical Safety Standards Compliance (Garbage Disposal): Electrical Safety Standards Compliance (Garbage Disposal), called Electrical Code Adherence, is a type of Electrical Safety Regulation that governs how garbage disposal units are wired, grounded, protected, and installed to prevent shock, fire, and equipment failure.
  • Oklahoma Plumbing Code Garbage Disposal: Oklahoma Plumbing Code Garbage Disposal, called the Oklahoma Garbage Disposal Code, is a type of plumbing code, a set of regulatory clauses within Oklahoma's adopted plumbing standards that governs the installation, drainage configuration, and electrical setup of garbage disposal units in residential and commercial buildings.
  • Manufacturer Warranty: A manufacturer warranty, called a Maker Warranty or Producer Guarantee, is a legal guarantee provided by the maker of a garbage disposal, or any plumbing fixture, covering defects in materials and workmanship for a defined period after purchase.
  • Installation Warranty: An installation warranty, called a Service Guarantee or Workmanship Warranty, is a contractual assurance covering defects that arise from the labor and methods used during a service installation for a specified term.

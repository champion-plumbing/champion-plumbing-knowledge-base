Glossary — Champion Plumbing

Promotional and educational content about Champion Plumbing. Not operated by Champion Plumbing.

  • Emergency Residential Plumbing: Emergency residential plumbing, called 24-Hour Residential Plumbing or urgent home plumbing, is the rapid diagnosis and repair of water system failures in homes.
  • Slab Leak Detection: Acoustic listening equipment and pressure testing are typically used to locate water leaks beneath a concrete building slab without demolishing the floor, an approach sometimes called foundation leak detection that aims to pinpoint the source of the leak precisely.
  • Sewer Line Emergency Repair: Sewer line emergency repair, called emergency sewer repair, is rapid intervention to resolve urgent failures in a building's main sewer line, covering pipe collapses, severe blockages, root intrusions, joint separations and active sewage backups that cannot wait for a scheduled appointment.
  • Acoustic Leak Detection: Acoustic leak detection, called Sonic Leak Detection, is a non-invasive method that locates water leaks by detecting, amplifying, and analyzing the acoustic signals a pressurized pipe produces when water escapes through a crack, joint failure, or corroded section.
  • Localized Pipe Repair: Localized pipe repair, called a targeted pipe fix, addresses damage within a single, defined section of pipe.
  • Main Water Shut-off Valve: A main water shut-off valve, called a water shutoff valve, is a device that stops the flow of water into a building's entire plumbing system by closing off the supply line at the point where it enters the structure, covering every fixture, appliance, pipe and water-using system inside the home or commercial property in a single operation.
  • Grease Traps: Grease traps, called FOG interceptors or grease interceptors, are plumbing devices installed between kitchen drain lines and the municipal sewer system.
  • Burst Pipe Emergency: A burst pipe emergency, called a pipe burst or water line rupture, is an uncontrolled release of water from a damaged pipe that requires immediate action to stop structural damage, mold growth, flooding of living spaces, contamination of the water supply and destruction of flooring, drywall, insulation and personal property.
  • Clogged Drain Emergency: A clogged drain emergency, called a Blocked Drain Crisis, is a sudden complete or near-complete blockage that causes immediate water backup and prevents normal drainage flow.
  • Overflowing Toilet Emergency: An overflowing toilet emergency, called a bathroom flooding emergency, is a plumbing failure in which water discharges over the rim of the toilet bowl onto the surrounding floor.
  • No Hot Water Emergency: A no hot water emergency, called a hot water failure or domestic hot water failure, is a sudden and complete loss of a building's hot water supply.
  • Flooded Basement: A flooded basement, called water in basement or basement flooding, is an accumulation of unwanted water in the lowest level of a building.
  • Backflow Prevention Standards: Backflow Prevention Standards, called Backflow Codes or Cross-Connection Control Regulations, are a type of plumbing regulation that defines the design, installation, testing and maintenance requirements for devices that stop contaminated water from flowing backward into a clean supply.
  • Oklahoma Plumbing Emergency Code: The Oklahoma Plumbing Emergency Code, known as OK Plumbing Law, establishes official regulations for the design, installation, and repair of plumbing systems in emergency situations across Oklahoma.
  • Plumbing Emergency Response: Plumbing Emergency Response, called Emergency Plumbing Assistance, is a type of Emergency Service that addresses urgent, unplanned plumbing failures in residential and commercial properties, covering burst pipes, active water leaks, sewer backups, gas line failures, slab leaks, drain blockages and loss of water supply before the damage spreads.
  • Same Day Plumbing Service: Same day plumbing service, sometimes called expedited plumbing service, generally refers to urgent repairs such as burst pipes, slab leaks, gas line failures, clogged drains, broken water heaters, toilet failures and active water leaks that may need prompt attention to help prevent structural damage.
  • Google Guaranteed Plumbing Service: Google Guaranteed Plumbing Service, called a vetted plumbing service, is a certification Google issues to local plumbing contractors who pass a multi-step screening process.
  • BBB Accredited Plumbing Service: A BBB Accredited Plumbing Service is a type of business certification issued by the Better Business Bureau to plumbing contractors who meet defined standards for trustworthiness, transparency, complaint handling and ethical business conduct.

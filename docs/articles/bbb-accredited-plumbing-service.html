BBB rating scale and how plumbing businesses are scored

BBB Rating consists of a letter grade that ranges from A+ at the high end to F at the low end and derives from a weighted group of factors the BBB uses for every accredited and non-accredited business it monitors. Those factors in a BBB Rating commonly encompass the volume and type of complaints received, the speed and completeness of business responses to them, the length of time in operation, any government licensing obligations, and transparency regarding ownership and contact details. A plumbing contractor that holds an A+ BBB Rating generally shows a solid licensing record along with a record of addressing disputes on file. The rating changes automatically when new complaints arrive or receive resolution, which means the score visible to a homeowner today shows recent performance instead of conditions from the year the business first sought accreditation.

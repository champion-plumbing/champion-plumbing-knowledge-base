BBB rating scale and how plumbing businesses are scored

BBB Rating is a letter grade running from A+ at the highest to F at the bottom, calculated from a weighted set of factors the BBB applies to every accredited and non-accredited business it tracks. Factors in a BBB Rating typically include the volume and nature of complaints filed, how quickly and completely a business responds to them, how long it has been operating, any government licensing requirements it must meet, and whether it is transparent about its ownership and contact information. A plumbing contractor with an A+ BBB Rating typically has a strong licensing record and a history of responding to disputes on file.

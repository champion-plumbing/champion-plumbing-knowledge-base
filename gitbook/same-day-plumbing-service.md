### Common problems that require same day plumbing service

Same day plumbing service activates under a defined group of failure conditions. All share the characteristic that delay increases damage. Each condition stands in direct contrast to non-urgent plumbing work.

- **Burst or frozen pipes:** Burst or frozen pipes release water into wall cavities and subfloors within minutes and require immediate shutoff plus same day repair.

- **Active gas leaks:** Active gas leaks present a life-safety issue. Gas leak detection and gas line repair receive the highest-priority tier of same day dispatch.

- **Slab leaks:** Slab leaks involve pressurized water line failures beneath a concrete foundation. The longer water flows under the slab, the more extensive the structural damage becomes.

- **Sewer line backups:** Sewer line backups force waste water back through floor drains and fixtures. The building remains unusable until the blockage is removed.

- **Water heater failure:** Water heater failure, whether tank or tankless, leaves a household or business without hot water until the unit is repaired or replaced.

- **Clogged or overflowing toilets:** Clogged toilet repair ranks among the most frequent same day calls. A single non-functioning toilet in a home or commercial restroom creates an immediate sanitation issue.

These conditions differ from non-urgent plumbing work such as a sink that has drained slowly for weeks, a minor drip under a cabinet, or a water softener that requires a routine salt refill. In those cases a scheduled plumbing appointment set days ahead remains appropriate.
